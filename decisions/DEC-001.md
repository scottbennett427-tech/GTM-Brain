## Rationale

1. The customer's stated problem (CALL-001) is preparation from scattered
   notes and outdated product information.
2. The customer asked to see exactly this: call notes becoming a preparation
   brief (CALL-001).
3. CAP-001 is an approved capability that matches that request (PROD-001 v1).
4. AlderWorks uses Salesforce (CRM-001), and the open Salesforce question
   must get the approved answer: not native today, manual export works
   (PROD-001 v1). Using the export in the demo answers it honestly.
